Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 18, 2026

Summary: YT Lite is a privacy-focused interface. We do not track your viewing habits or sell your data. We use Google OAuth solely to verify your account and sync your subscriptions locally.

1. Introduction

Welcome to YT Lite ("we," "our," or "us"). We are committed to protecting your personal information and your right to privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights in relation to it.

2. Information We Collect

We only collect the minimum information necessary to provide our service:

  • Google Account Information: When you log in via Google, we receive your email address and basic profile information to verify your identity.
  • YouTube Data: We access your YouTube subscription list solely to display your personalized feed. This data is derived directly from YouTube via the API.
  • Local Usage Data: Your preferences (like blocked channels or custom navigation) are stored locally on your own instance or device.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Authenticate your access to the application.
  • Synchronize your YouTube subscriptions.
  • Provide a personalized viewing experience (e.g., hiding blocked channels).

We do not share, sell, or rent your personal information to third parties.

4. Data Storage and Security

Your session tokens are stored securely. We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your data. However, please remember that no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure.

5. Third-Party Services

This application interacts with:

  • Google/YouTube API Services: Used for authentication and data retrieval. Please refer to the Google Privacy Policy.
  • Invidious Instances: We use Invidious APIs to fetch video metadata without tracking.

6. Contact Us

If you have questions about this policy, please contact us at support@ytlite.app.
